What is HNTR.art?

HNTR is a next-generation Web3 platform that combines co-owned NFT pools with a powerful referral system.

The platform rewards word-of-mouth sharing and referrals, driving massive adoption by bringing hundreds of thousands of users into the NFT ecosystem.

What problem does HNTR solve?

Blue-chip NFTs are among the most traded digital assets in the world, but most people can only watch. A single Bored Ape has sold for as much as $450,000. A CryptoPunk peaked at $500,000. Even at today's lower floors, one piece still costs more than most people can spend alone.

Three barriers keep normal people out

  • Price. The entry ticket to a top collection is a five or six-figure purchase.

  • Risk. Going alone means unfamiliar wallets, marketplaces and scams, with no one to share the cost of a mistake.

Where does HNTR fit into all this?

Buying the right NFT at the right price and flipping it well is a full-time skill — and blue-chip pieces cost more than most people can spend alone. HNTR solves both. We run NFT strategy pools — one per major blue-chip collection — where hunters co-own and flip pieces together. You become a fractional owner of premium collections, and permissionless smart contracts do the rest.

The strategy — a perpetual cycle

  1. You deposit. Add funds to an active strategy for a collection you like. That's the whole job.
  2. It buys at the floor. The smart contract finds and buys an underpriced floor NFT automatically.
  3. It flips at a premium. The piece is relisted and sold at a markup, typically 10–25% — no clicking from you.
  4. You claim. Your deposit plus your share of the profit is returned to everyone in the pool.
  5. It restarts. A new strategy launches instantly and the cycle repeats — always in motion.

So you get exposure to blue-chip NFT trading — collections like CryptoPunks, BAYC, Azuki, and Pudgy Penguins — without picking pieces, timing the market, or babysitting listings. You deposit; the strategy pools, buys, flips, distributes, and restarts on its own.
NFT Strategy

How does an NFT strategy work?

An NFT strategy is a pool for one collection only. Hunters deposit into it together. When the pool reaches its target, it buys the cheapest NFT listed in that collection, relists it at a higher price, and pays everyone back when it sells. Then it starts again.

One loop, fully on-chain

  1. Deposit. A pool opens on the cheapest listed NFT in the collection. Members deposit ETH until the target is reached.
  2. Buy and list. The moment the target is reached, the NFT is bought and immediately listed at a premium.
  3. Sale. When it sells, 100% of every deposit is returned in ETH, plus each member's share of the profit.
  4. Repeat. The pool reopens on the next cheapest listing, and the cycle continues.

NFT Strategy

The spinwheel and the flywheel

Two effects keep strategies moving without the wider market needing to climb.

The spinwheel: pools buy from each other

Each pool buys the cheapest listing in the collection. When that cheapest listing is an NFT HNTR already holds, the next pool buys it back from the earlier pool's members. The earlier pool is paid out with capital plus profit, and the same NFT moves on to its next owners.

  1. Pool #1 buys NFT A and lists it.
  2. Pools #2 to #5 buy NFTs B to E and list them.
  3. Pool #6 buys NFT A from Pool #1, releasing Pool #1's capital and profit.
  4. Pool #7 buys NFT B from Pool #2, and so on.

The flywheel: growth feeds growth

More people
More Hunters join and deposit into pools. The bigger the group, the more it can buy.
More buying
Pools buy the cheapest NFTs in blue-chip collections.
Higher floors
As the cheapest listings are absorbed, the floor rises, and the NFTs already held are worth more.
In one line

More people → more NFTs bought → prices go up → more people want in.
